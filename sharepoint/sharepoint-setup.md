# SharePoint Setup Guide

**By Blair Page**

## Suggested page

**Page title:** Mentor Starter Kit  
**Description:** A supportive space for goal-setting, reflective conversations, skill development, and accountable follow-through.

### Recommended web parts

1. **Hero:** “Build confidence. Share knowledge. Turn goals into action.”
2. **Text:** purpose, principles, and how to use the kit.
3. **Quick Links:** Mentoring Agreement, Goal & Action Plan, Session Agenda, Session Notes, Progress Review, and Resource Library.
4. **Document Library:** approved templates and learning resources with clear version names.
5. **Microsoft Lists (optional):** track goals, actions, owners, due dates, status, and review dates. Keep sensitive details out of broadly accessible lists.
6. **Calendar or Events:** link to Outlook or Teams scheduling.
7. **FAQ:** confidentiality, expectations, rescheduling, and how to request additional support.

## Governance checklist

- [ ] Assign a content owner and review date.
- [ ] Keep general resources separate from private mentoring records.
- [ ] Restrict individual records to the mentor, mentee, and authorized administrators.
- [ ] Do not store credentials, sensitive personal data, customer data, or confidential employer information in shared templates.
- [ ] Use accessible headings, descriptive link text, readable contrast, and alt text for meaningful images.
- [ ] Review links, permissions, and templates at least quarterly.
- [ ] Use clear version names, for example `Mentoring-Agreement-v1.0.docx`.

## Suggested welcome copy

Welcome to the Mentor Starter Kit. This space helps mentors and mentees build a purposeful, respectful, and practical development relationship. Start by agreeing on one meaningful goal, choosing a meeting rhythm, and completing the mentoring agreement. Use the session agenda to keep conversations focused, the action plan to turn insights into practice, and the progress review to recognize growth and adjust direction. The mentee owns their goals and decisions; the mentor offers questions, perspective, encouragement, and relevant experience. Keep shared records professional, minimal, and appropriately permissioned.

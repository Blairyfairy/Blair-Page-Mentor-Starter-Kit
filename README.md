# Mentor Starter Kit
**By Blair Page**  
*Coaching • Career Growth • Practical Knowledge Sharing*

A practical, people-first mentoring framework for setting goals, sharing experience, building confidence, and turning conversations into achievable next steps. Designed to be adapted for Microsoft SharePoint, Microsoft Teams, or any mentoring program.

## Purpose

This starter kit helps mentors and mentees establish a respectful working relationship, clarify development goals, structure useful conversations, and review progress over time. It emphasizes curiosity, active listening, practical problem-solving, inclusive communication, and mentee ownership.

## What's Included

- `docs/mentoring-framework.md` — mentoring principles, conversation prompts, and an eight-session pathway.
- `templates/mentoring-agreement.md` — a reusable agreement covering goals, cadence, feedback, boundaries, and confidentiality.
- `templates/session-agenda.md` — a repeatable 30–45 minute meeting structure.
- `templates/goal-action-plan.md` — a simple goal, action, evidence, due-date, and status tracker.
- `templates/session-notes.md` — notes for insights, options, decisions, and follow-up actions.
- `templates/progress-review.md` — reflective check-in and development review.
- `sharepoint/sharepoint-setup.md` — suggested SharePoint page structure, library layout, and governance checklist.

## Getting Started

1. Read the [Mentoring Framework](docs/mentoring-framework.md).
2. Customize the [Mentoring Agreement](templates/mentoring-agreement.md) together.
3. Choose one meaningful development goal and record it in the [Goal & Action Plan](templates/goal-action-plan.md).
4. Use the [Session Agenda](templates/session-agenda.md) for each meeting.
5. Capture only appropriate professional notes using the [Session Notes](templates/session-notes.md) template.
6. Revisit the [Progress Review](templates/progress-review.md) every few sessions and adapt the plan.

## Suggested SharePoint Setup

Create a SharePoint communication or team site page titled **Mentor Starter Kit**. Add a welcome section, quick links to the templates, a document library for approved resources, and—if appropriate—a Microsoft List for goals and action items. Keep private mentoring records separate from general learning materials and restrict access to the people who need it.

## Core Principles

- Listen first and understand context before offering advice.
- Ask whether the mentee wants questions, options, practice, or direct feedback.
- Turn broad ambitions into small, observable actions.
- Explain the reasoning behind recommendations and invite adaptation.
- Respect different backgrounds, communication styles, access needs, and career paths.
- Agree on confidentiality and its limits; never promise absolute confidentiality when law, policy, or safety requires escalation.
- Keep records professional, minimal, and appropriately permissioned.
- Recognize learning and thoughtful effort—not only outcomes.

## Conversation Themes

The framework includes prompts for:

- Technical problem-solving and systems thinking
- Cloud, infrastructure, reliability, and operations
- Troubleshooting and customer support
- Communication and stakeholder trust
- Leadership and mentoring
- Product thinking and customer value
- Career planning and continuous learning
- Prioritization, resilience, and follow-through

## Responsible Use

This repository is a general-purpose mentoring resource. Adapt it to your organization's policies and the needs of the participants. Do not store passwords, credentials, sensitive personal information, customer data, or confidential employer information in mentoring notes or public repositories. Confirm access permissions before sharing private records.

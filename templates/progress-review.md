# Progress Review Template

**By Blair Page**

Complete every three or four sessions, or at an agreed midpoint. Use evidence and reflection rather than ranking people.

## Review prompts

1. What progress are you most proud of?
2. What evidence shows a skill, habit, or decision has developed?
3. What remains difficult or uncertain?
4. Have your priorities or circumstances changed?
5. Do you feel heard, respected, and able to disagree?
6. What should we stop, start, or continue?
7. What will you own independently next?

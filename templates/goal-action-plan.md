# Goal and Action Plan

**By Blair Page**

Use one copy per development goal. Keep goals specific, realistic, and connected to something the mentee values.

## Example

**Goal:** By the next review, explain one troubleshooting case using a concise problem–evidence–options–recommendation format and request feedback from a peer.

**Evidence:** A short written summary and one piece of feedback.

## Reflection

- What did I learn?
- What changed after I took action?
- What is the next smallest useful step?

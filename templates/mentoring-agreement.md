- **Preparation expectations:** Each person brings updates, questions, or a topic when possible.
- **Feedback preference:** Ask whether the mentee wants reflective questions, options, candid feedback, or practice.
- **Confidentiality:** Conversations stay within the agreed circle except where disclosure is required by law, policy, safety, or agreed escalation procedures. Do not place sensitive personal or organizational data in shared notes.
- **Recordkeeping:** Agree what will be written down, where it will be stored, and who can access it.
- **Boundaries:** Mentoring supports development; it does not replace management responsibilities, formal HR processes, therapy, legal advice, or specialized professional advice.
- **Review or exit:** Either person may revisit goals, pause, or end the arrangement respectfully.

## Shared commitment

We will communicate respectfully, prepare as reasonably possible, make space for questions and disagreement, and revisit the plan when circumstances change.

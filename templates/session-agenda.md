# Session Agenda Template

**By Blair Page**  
Suggested duration: 30–45 minutes.

1. **Check-in — 5 minutes**  
   What has changed since the last conversation?

2. **Action review — 5 minutes**  
   What was completed, is in progress, is blocked, or is no longer relevant?

3. **Focus topic — 15–20 minutes**  
   Explore the situation, evidence, assumptions, options, and trade-offs.

4. **Next actions — 5–10 minutes**  
   Choose one or two actions, define evidence of completion, set due dates, and identify support needed.

5. **Close — 3–5 minutes**  
   What was most useful? What should we do differently next time?

## Optional opening questions

- What would make today's conversation useful?
- What are you thinking about most right now?
- Would you prefer questions, ideas, direct feedback, or practice today?
